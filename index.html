Process

From first call to live software.

Every project follows the same four steps, so you always know where it stands and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    A 30 minute call about what you need, who will use it and what it has to connect to. No charge, and no obligation afterwards.

  2. 02

    Fixed proposal

    A written scope with a fixed price and timeline, broken into milestones. You pay per milestone, so you never pay for work you have not seen.

  3. 03

    Build in your repository

    Each feature is built on its own branch and reviewed before it merges. You get a staging link to try every change before it goes live.

    feature branches · code review · weekly staging release
  4. 04

    Launch and handover

    We deploy to your domain and hand over the code, documentation and access. Keep us on a support retainer or take it in-house. Either way, it is yours.

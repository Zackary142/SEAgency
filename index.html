Process

From first chat to launch day.

Every project follows the same four steps, so you always know where it stands and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Free call

    A 30 minute chat about what you need and who will use it. No charge, and no obligation afterwards.

  2. 02

    Fixed price

    A written plan with a fixed price and timeline, split into stages. You pay as each stage is done, so you never pay for work you have not seen.

  3. 03

    We build, you preview

    We build in small pieces, check each one twice, and send you a preview link so you can try every change before it goes live.

    small steps · checked twice · weekly preview
  4. 04

    Launch and handover

    We put it live on your website and hand over everything: the code, instructions and logins. Keep us on for support or take it in-house. Either way, it is yours.
